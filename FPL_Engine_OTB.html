Real player news and market movement, pulled from official FPL data your Worker has already been tracking. There is no structured data for preseason friendlies anywhere — FPL only tracks the 38-gameweek league season — so this surfaces the two honest signals that DO exist: official status/news updates, and price moves, which reflect thousands of managers reacting to real information before it always makes the news.
Every club's upcoming run, easiest first. Attack rates how leaky the opponent is — use it for forwards and attacking mids. Clean sheet rates how likely your defence keeps one — use it for defenders and keepers. They are genuinely different: a side can be great to attack against while also scoring freely.
Build a squad by tapping players in the pool, then save it here under a name. Saved squads are kept in this browser and survive data refreshes — players are matched by identity, not by list position, so a save still loads correctly after prices change.

The solver reselects the best legal XI, formation and captain separately for every gameweek in the chosen horizon, then optimises the 15-player squad against the combined result. It uses a hybrid expected-points forecast: historical prior, current underlying statistics, recent form, official FPL forecast and current availability. Click any player’s xPts number or the ⓘ button to inspect the calculation and override uncertain minutes.
Searches a large set of candidates and improves on them with swaps until nothing helps. Each gameweek receives its own legal XI and captain. The result is strong, but remains a heuristic rather than proof of the global optimum.

OTB hybrid xPts. Historical output is retained as a prior, not multiplied again by old minutes or the player’s own club strength. Current-season xG, xA, expected minutes, clean-sheet probability, saves, goals-conceded deductions, cards, DefCon threshold probability and bonus are introduced gradually as evidence accumulates. Official FPL xPts and recent form are calibration layers, not extra points added on top. Bonus specifically also carries a 2026/27 BPS rule adjustment on the historical prior — CBI-reliant defenders are scaled down (clearances/blocks/interceptions now earn less per action), goalkeepers and attacking players scaled up slightly (tackled-suffered penalty removed; save scoring enhanced) — since last season’s bonus rate was earned under different rules. This correction fades out automatically as real 2026/27 bonus data accumulates.
