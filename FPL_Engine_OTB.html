OTB hybrid xPts. Historical output is retained as a prior, not multiplied again by old minutes or the player’s own club strength. Current-season xG, xA, expected minutes, clean-sheet probability, saves, goals-conceded deductions, cards, DefCon threshold probability and bonus are introduced gradually as evidence accumulates. Official FPL xPts and recent form are calibration layers, not extra points added on top. Bonus specifically also carries a 2026/27 BPS rule adjustment on the historical prior — CBI-reliant defenders are scaled down (clearances/blocks/interceptions now earn less per action), goalkeepers and attacking players scaled up slightly (tackled-suffered penalty removed; save scoring enhanced) — since last season’s bonus rate was earned under different rules. This correction fades out automatically as real 2026/27 bonus data accumulates.
