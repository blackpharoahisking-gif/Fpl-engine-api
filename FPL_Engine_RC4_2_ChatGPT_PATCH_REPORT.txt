ADDITIONAL MODEL DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED
- Seed-mode home/away attack and defence ratings stored as null were being coerced to numeric zero rather than falling back to club strength.
- This flattened fixture differences across clubs and affected xPts.
- ratingValue() now falls back to the club strength whenever a live split rating is missing, invalid or non-positive.
